RAMS Builder

Pick the work, tick the site conditions, add the site details. You’ll get a Word RAMS with only the sections that apply, the pre-installation checklist for the client to complete, or both.

Content comes from Gaia’s existing 27 RAMS templates and 11 pre-installation checklists and is still being reviewed. Every document is marked Draft and needs the Operations Manager’s approval before it goes to site.

1What work is being done?

Choose one or more. Combine, for example, a hydronic floor downstairs with an electric mat in the bathroom. Systems marked “Checklist” also have a pre-installation checklist.

2What else applies on this site?

Sections already required by the chosen work are ticked and can’t be removed.

3Site details

Anything left blank appears as a highlighted gap to fill in on the document.
